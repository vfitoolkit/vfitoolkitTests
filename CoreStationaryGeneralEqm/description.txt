Core Stationary General Eqm tests.

Baseline model: Aiyagari-style incomplete-markets economy with endogenous
labor and a government. Household state (a,z), decision d=hours, choice
aprime. Firm is Cobb-Douglas. The wage w is hardcoded from r via the firm FOC
(w=(1-alpha)*A*((r+delta)/(alpha*A))^(alpha/(alpha-1))) wherever it is needed,
so w is NOT a GE price. The government has two budgets: a fixed labor income
tax rate tau funds a lump-sum transfer Tr (Tr adjusts), and a consumption tax
tau_c funds exogenous spending G (tau_c adjusts).

Three general eqm prices: r, Tr, tau_c.
Three general eqm eqns:
  CapitalMarket: r = alpha*A*(K/N)^(alpha-1) - delta
  GovBudget:     tau*w*N = Tr
  ConsTax:       tau_c*C = G

Tests, run for both InfHorz and FHorz (two halves of CoreStationaryGeneralEqmTests.m):
  (ii)  solve with fminalgo=1 (fminsearch), 5 (shooting), 8 (lsqnonlin),
        4 (CMA-ES); confirm all give the same equilibrium prices (CMA-ES is
        stochastic/lower-accuracy, so a looser match). After the fminalgo=1
        solve, also compute V and the StationaryDist and plot the cumulative
        distribution over the asset grid.
  (iii) re-solve applying each of the three parameter-constraint kinds, one per
        price: constrain0to1 on r, constrainpositive on Tr, constrainAtoB on
        tau_c (limits [0,1]), and then all three at once; confirm each
        reproduces the unconstrained equilibrium (all equilibria are interior,
        so the constraints are slack).

The same four tests are then repeated with permanent types (PType): N_i=2 types
that differ in the value of sigma (2.2 and 1.8), equal mass 0.5 each. The GE
prices (r,Tr,tau_c) remain economy-wide. Outputs: output1..output4 for the
non-PType tests, output1ptype..output4ptype for the PType ones.

Commands exercised: HeteroAgentStationaryEqm_InfHorz, HeteroAgentStationaryEqm_Case1_FHorz,
HeteroAgentStationaryEqm_InfHorz_PType, HeteroAgentStationaryEqm_Case1_FHorz_PType.
